# 🏏 Dream11 Fantasy Sports Case Study  

## 📌 Problem Statement  
**How can we enhance user trust in Dream11's cricket contests to drive user acquisition, retention, and revenue growth while addressing social stigma?**  

## 🚀 Why Dream11?  
- Holds **90% market share** in the fantasy sports industry.  
- Faces **high risk of customer loss** due to competition.  
- Cricket betting contributes **85% of Dream11’s revenue**.  

## 🔍 Research & Analysis  
To identify trust-related issues, we conducted:  
- **6 User Interviews** 🎤  
- **100+ Play Store Review Analyses** ⭐  
- **30+ Articles & Video Analyses** 📄  
- **2 Usability Tests** 🧑‍💻  

## 🔑 Key Challenges Identified  
### 1️⃣ Crowded Sports Fantasy Arena  
- Over **50 competing apps** make differentiation difficult.  

### 2️⃣ Market Penetration Challenges  
- **70% of the market remains untapped**, with no clear strategy for expansion.  

### 3️⃣ Social Stigma  
- Fantasy sports participants are often seen as **obsessive and disconnected from reality**.  

### 4️⃣ Fairness Concerns  
- Users feel that **game outcomes are influenced by uncontrollable factors** rather than skill.  

## 🧠 Our Process  
### **PHASE 1: Define**  
- Identified key industry challenges requiring immediate attention.  

### **PHASE 2: Brainstorm**  
- Explored factors that contribute to **trust in fantasy sports apps**.  

### **PHASE 3: Problem Identification**  
- Pinpointed **trust issues** as the root cause affecting user experience.  

### **PHASE 4: Improve**  
- Developed **user-generated content (UGC) strategies** to enhance emotional connection & trust.  

## 📌 Our Proposed Solution: **User-Generated Content (UGC) Strategy**  
A **cost-effective marketing strategy** that promotes trust through real user experiences.  

### 🎯 Expected Benefits  
✅ Increased **brand exposure** through testimonials shared on social media.  
✅ Improved **credibility & trust** by leveraging real user experiences.  
✅ Boosted **new user acquisition** through word-of-mouth promotion.  
✅ Strengthened **brand loyalty & customer satisfaction** with user engagement rewards.  
✅ Valuable **feedback for continuous improvement** in product & marketing.
